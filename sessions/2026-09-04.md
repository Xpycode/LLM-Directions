# Session: 2026-09-04

## Goal

Critically review Directions for a period of primarily using Codex, grounding the review in the ten
most recently active consumer projects and turning the highest-confidence gaps into a first-class
Codex workflow.

## Evidence reviewed

- Selected the ten most recently active projects by the latest modification time of
  `docs/sessions/*.md`, falling back to `docs/PROJECT_STATE.md`: MousePlus, VideoCorruptor,
  PlayPlayPlay, Group Alarms, CameraTranscoder, SAR, zPackages, QuickStatsPanel, PRIVAT, and Penumbra.
- Read their current state, recent logs, and root agent entry points. All ten use recognizable
  Directions practices; none has a root `AGENTS.md`. Projects with an entry point route only through
  `CLAUDE.md`; Group Alarms and zPackages have neither root entry point.
- Checked current official Codex guidance for global/project `AGENTS.md`, user/repository skills,
  progressive disclosure, hooks, and the deprecation of custom prompts in favor of skills.

## What held up

- Lean `PROJECT_STATE.md` plus detailed session history gives fast orientation without losing
  evidence.
- Wave plans, independent review, hard build/test/manual gates, exact resume points, and honest
  blocker recording are repeatedly useful in real work.
- The read-on-demand master avoids the copied-doc drift seen in QuickStatsPanel and the stale-skill
  false verdicts seen in zPackages.
- Multi-Mac safeguards remain load-bearing: several logs record Syncthing sediment, stale branches,
  or false-clean/false-dirty states that could otherwise become duplicate or destructive commits.

## Gaps found

- Codex had no documented install or refresh path; the live adapter was a manual symlink in a legacy
  location.
- `/directions update`, the generated topic router, and lifecycle automation were Claude-only.
- Claude model-marker logic lived inside shared commands and depended on the adapter remembering to
  ignore it.
- Existing Directions projects were considered "done" by setup even when Codex lacked `AGENTS.md`.
- QuickStatsPanel and CameraTranscoder logs showed that a green source tree is insufficient when the
  archive or installed app predates the final fix.

## Decision

Keep one shared command library with thin host adapters. Use a standalone Codex skill plus global
`AGENTS.md` while the workflow is still being exercised personally. Do not package a plugin or port
Codex hooks until real-session behavior justifies their additional trust and maintenance surface.
